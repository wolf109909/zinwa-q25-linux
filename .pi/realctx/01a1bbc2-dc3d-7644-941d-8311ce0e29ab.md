When repacking `boot-7.3.3-cachyos-q25-*.img` (64 MiB `ANDROID!` boot image) on Zinwa Q25 (`MT6789`), the AVB footer (`AVB0` magic at byte offset `34283520`) MUST be preserved in-place (`orig[k_off:34283520] = ...`), and the initramfs must be packed with `cpio -o -H newc -R 0:0` after `chmod 0755 bin/mount`. Zero-filling the tail past `34283520` wipes the `AVB0` descriptor and causes the Q25 LK bootloader to boot-loop before loading the kernel.
