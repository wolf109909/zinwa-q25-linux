On the Zinwa Q25 (MediaTek MT6789), the onboard sensors are attached to two I2C buses that the bootloader leaves in SCP pinmux mode (mode 2):

1. **GPIO132 (SCL1 / SCP_SCL0) & GPIO133 (SDA1 / SCP_SDA0)** (`GPIO_MODE` offset `0x400` bits `[23:16]`):
   - **Silan SC7A20 3-axis Accelerometer** at I2C address `0x18`:
     - `WHO_AM_I` (`0x0f`) = `0x11`.
     - Init: `CTRL_REG1` (`0x20`) = `0x57` (100 Hz, X/Y/Z enabled), `CTRL_REG4` (`0x23`) = `0x88` (BDU=1, high-resolution ±2g).
     - Read: 6 bytes starting at `0x28 | 0x80` (`0xa8`, bit 7 enables multi-byte address auto-increment; without `0x80` multi-byte reads do not increment). Little-endian signed 16-bit `(ax, ay, az)` where `1g = 16384` LSB (`9.80665 / 16384 m/s²` per LSB).
   - **MEMSIC MMC5603NJ 3-axis Magnetometer** at I2C address `0x30`:
     - `PRODUCT_ID` (`0x39`) = `0x10`.
     - Trigger measurement: write `0x21` (`TM_M | Auto_SR_en`) to `CTRL0` (`0x1b`).
     - Read: 6 bytes at `0x00..0x05` (`Xout0, Xout1, Yout0, Yout1, Zout0, Zout1`, big-endian 16-bit unsigned with midpoint `32768`, sensitivity `1024 LSB/Gauss` = `10.24 LSB/µT`).

2. **GPIO148 (SCL9 / SCP_SCL2) & GPIO149 (SDA9 / SCP_SDA2)** (`GPIO_MODE` offset `0x420` bits `[23:16]`):
   - **TYHX HX32062SE Ambient Light (ALS) & Proximity (PS) Sensor** at I2C address `0x2b`:
     - Chip ID at `0x00` = `0x37`.
     - 64-entry register init table (`0x64=0x00 .. 0x70=0x00`, keeping `0xef=0x9f`, `0x29=0x06`, `0x39=0x06`, `0x4a=0x06`), followed by `0x45=0x10`, `0x1d=0x10`.
     - Conversion trigger sequence: write `0x4c = 0x0f` (standby), `0x1a = 0x01`, `0x1a = 0x05`, then `0x4c = 0x00` (active). Wait ~100 ms.
     - Read data: latch with `0x83 = 0x02`, read `0x01..0x06` (two 24-bit little-endian ALS channels `ALS0`, `ALS1`, left-aligned by 6 bits; dark baseline on `ALS0 >> 6` is ~46800) and `0x0d..0x0f` (24-bit little-endian PS count, or 16-bit at `0x69..0x6a`; unobstructed ~75, near threshold 3800 / far threshold 3300), then unlatch with `0x83 = 0x00`.
