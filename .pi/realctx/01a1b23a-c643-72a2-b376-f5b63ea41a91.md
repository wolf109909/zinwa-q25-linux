The Zinwa Q25 (`Xelex` / `q20_v12_factory`, MediaTek Helio G99 `MT6789`) kernel and driver sources are available from:
- LineageOS full GPL kernel and in-tree/out-of-tree modules: `https://github.com/LineageOS/android_kernel_xelex_mt6789` (branch `lineage-23.2`, Linux 5.10.246+).
- Jami Kettunen's full-source kernel + modules repo: `https://github.com/JamiKettunen/android_kernel_zinwa_mt6789` (default branch `droidian-new`, updated March 2026), based on the LineageOS repo's `lineage-23.2` branch. Additions in `droidian-new`: modern Clang/LLD Kbuild backports (`db15c8aa`..`250ef819`); `epoll_pwait2` and `close_range` syscall backports; `e91a8df5 gen4m: Add NL80211_WPA_VERSION_3 enumeration` (WPA3 support in MTK Wi-Fi driver); `d7c7f27b mali_valhall: fix obtaining pid struct when PID namespaces are used`; `7c9182fa accdet: report both headphone and mic insert for 4-pole connector`; `997f55c5 droidian.config: enable previously GKI ABI-breaking stuff` ("There's no reason to limit ourselves with full kernel source being used :)"); and the `lindroid-evdi` DRM driver (`CONFIG_DRM_LINDROID_EVDI=y`). Its defconfig chain is `gki_defconfig mgk.config entry_level.config q20_v12_factory.config droidian.config`.
- Device tree and module load lists (`modules.load`, `modules.load.ramdisk`, `modules.load.recovery`, `BoardConfig.mk`): `https://github.com/LineageOS/android_device_xelex_Q25` (branch `lineage-23.2`). The LineageOS kernel defconfig fragment chain is `gki_defconfig mgk.config entry_level.config q20_v12_factory.config` (`arch/arm64/configs/q20_v12_factory.config`).
- Key Q25 drivers in `LineageOS/android_kernel_xelex_mt6789`: keyboard and trackpad at `drivers/input/keyboard/bbqX0kbd/` and `drivers/input/keyboard/q20_keyboard.c` (`CONFIG_KEYBOARD_Q20=m`); touchscreen at `drivers/input/touchscreen/synaptics_dsx/` (`CONFIG_TOUCHSCREEN_MTK_SYNAPTICS_I2C_RMI4=m`, includes commit `2a873a35` fixing stuck touches); display panel at `drivers/gpu/drm/panel/panel-nt35596-boe-fhd-video.c` (`CONFIG_DRM_PANEL_NT35596_BOE_FHD_VIDEO=m`); MediaTek connectivity (Wi-Fi/BT/FM/GPS) at `drivers/misc/mediatek/connectivity/` (`wmt_drv.ko`, `connfem.ko`, `wlan_drv_gen4m_6789.ko`, `bt_drv_6789.ko`, `fmradio_drv_mt6631_6635.ko`, `gps_drv_stp.ko`).
- Jami Kettunen's U-Boot for MT6789: `https://github.com/JamiKettunen/u-boot-mt6789`; Droidian 5.10 kernel: `https://github.com/JamiKettunen/android_kernel_common-q25`.
