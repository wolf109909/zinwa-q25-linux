In `drivers/touchscreen/synaptics_dsx_core.c`, `synaptics_rmi4_set_params()` initializes multitouch slots with `INPUT_MT_DIRECT | INPUT_MT_DROP_UNUSED` and registers single-touch `ABS_X`/`ABS_Y`. However, `INPUT_MT_DIRECT` requires calling `input_mt_sync_frame(rmi4_data->input_dev)` before `input_sync()` in `synaptics_rmi4_f11_abs_report()`, `synaptics_rmi4_f12_abs_report()`, and `synaptics_rmi4_free_fingers()`; otherwise single-touch `ABS_X`/`ABS_Y` and `BTN_TOUCH`/`BTN_TOOL_FINGER` pointer emulation are never emitted, and unused slots are not dropped. Additionally, the default One Euro filter parameters (`min_cutoff=1000` (1 Hz), `deadzone=1000` (1 px)) cause severe lag and swallow small drag movements in GTK4/Phosh; use `one_euro_min_cutoff=5000`, `one_euro_beta=20000`, `one_euro_d_cutoff=5000`, and `one_euro_deadzone=0`. Also add `s2716b_ts` to `90-touchscreen.rules` in the initramfs so udev tags `ID_INPUT_TOUCHSCREEN=1`.
