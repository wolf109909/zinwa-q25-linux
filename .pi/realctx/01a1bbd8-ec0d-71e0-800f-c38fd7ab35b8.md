On Zinwa Q25 Linux 7.3.3, every PTY (foot, ssh shells, `os.openpty`) returned 0 on write/read and FIONREAD gave ENOTTY because `/proc/tty/ldiscs` showed `n_mtkstp 0`: `drivers/mediatek/connectivity/common/common_main/linux/stp_uart.c` memset()s `stp_uart_ldisc` and on >=5.15 calls `tty_register_ldisc(&stp_uart_ldisc)` with `.num` left 0, replacing N_TTY for all ttys opened after `wmt_drv.ko` loads. Fix: set `stp_uart_ldisc.num = N_MTKSTP;` (16) before registering. Verified: after fix `n_tty 0`, `n_mtkstp 16`, pty writes/reads work. The earlier "GCC 16 __builtin_counted_by_ref tty_buffer miscompile" theory and the q25-terminal subprocess.PIPE workaround were wrong/unnecessary. Diagnose with `cat /proc/tty/ldiscs`.
