MediaTek LK fastboot on Zinwa Q25 rejected a userdata sparse chunk containing a single ~8 GB zero FILL record with `FAILED (remote: 'sparse image write failure')`. Fix in build/mksparse-chunks.py: split zero runs into FILL records of at most 4096 blocks (16 MiB, MAX_FILL). Chunks are self-contained, so a failed chunk can be regenerated and reflashed alone (`fastboot.exe flash userdata userdata_NNN.simg`, then `set_active a`, `reboot`). Writing 8 GB of fill takes ~19 s on the phone.
