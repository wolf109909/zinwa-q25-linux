Arch Linux ARM glibc ld.so only searches /usr/lib by default; with no ld.so.cache in the initramfs, libraries copied to /lib are NOT found (e2fsck exited 127 'libext2fs.so.2: cannot open shared object', initramfs dropped to rescue: telnet 10.15.19.82:23). Fix (commit after 5880cf9): build/build-initramfs.sh puts libs in usr/lib with lib, lib64 -> usr/lib symlinks, plus a qemu-aarch64-static -L smoke test of e2fsck -V. Rescue workaround on device: mkdir -p /usr/lib; cp -a /lib/*.so* /usr/lib/; echo > /run/continue.
