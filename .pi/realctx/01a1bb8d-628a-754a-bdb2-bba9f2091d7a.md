On Zinwa Q25 (`MT6789`, Mainline Linux `7.3.3-cachyos-q25`):
1. **Dynamic `+EIF` -> `ccmniX` Binding in `q25-modem-init`:**
   - When `AT+EAPNACT=1,"ctnet","default",0` activates a PDN context, MD1 emits `+EIF: <if_id>, ifup, 3, <ipv4>, ...` where `<if_id>` increments across PDN cycles (`300`, `301`, `302`, `303`, ...).
   - MD1 binds `<if_id>` to `ccmni{if_id % 100}` (e.g., `302` -> `ccmni2`, `303` -> `ccmni3`) and requires `AT+EIF=<if_id>, "ifst", "up", 3` to mark that interface up in the modem's NetAgent table.
   - `userspace/sbin/q25-modem-init` parses `+EIF: (\d+), ifup` from the `AT+EAPNACT=1` response, sends `AT+EIF={if_id}, "ifst", "up", 3`, configures `ccmni{if_id % 100}` with the assigned IPv4 and default route, and reports any active `ccmni0..7` IPv4 in `q25-modem-init status`, `q25-control-center`, and `q25-status`.
2. **`SYSTEMD_IGNORE_CHROOT=1` in `q25-phosh-start`:**
   - Because `q25-phosh-start` runs inside `chroot /opt/arch`, `udevadm trigger` and `udevadm settle` silently print `Running in chroot, ignoring request.` unless `export SYSTEMD_IGNORE_CHROOT=1` is set.
   - Setting `export SYSTEMD_IGNORE_CHROOT=1` ensures `udevadm trigger` tags `/dev/input/event*` and `/dev/dri/card*` with `ID_SEAT=seat0` and `TAGS=:seat:` before `phoc` starts, preventing `libinput initialization failed, no input devices`.
3. **LVM Root Volume Renamed from `droidian` to `q25`:**
   - The UFS LVM volume group on `/dev/sdc60` is `q25` with logical volumes `q25-rootfs` (`/dev/mapper/q25-q25--rootfs`), `q25-persistent`, and `q25-reserved`.
   - `userspace/sbin/init-7.3.3-cachyos-q25` mounts `/dev/mapper/q25-q25--rootfs` at `/rootfs`, bind-mounts `/rootfs/opt/arch` at `/opt/arch`, and starts native `/opt/arch/usr/bin/keyd` directly without `droidian_minienv`.
